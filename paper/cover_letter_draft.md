Dear Editors of SoftwareX,

Please consider “RiskSelRUL for reproducible selective remaining useful life evaluation” as an Original Software Publication.

RiskSelRUL combines a reproducible sensor-fault benchmark with reusable NumPy interfaces for acceptance and rejection evaluation. The software supports grouped training, controlled fault injection, calibrated thresholds, fixed-count comparisons and saved-table checking. Researchers can use the full RUL workflow or evaluate prediction arrays from their own models.

The manuscript follows the official SoftwareX template and provides executable input-to-output examples, an interface comparison with related software, automated tests and numerical validation records. The public 0.1.1.dev0 candidate passes 21 installed-package tests on Ubuntu and Windows/Python 3.10 and 3.12. Fresh remote and local runs additionally execute the complete raw-data workflow for both supported engine subsets. It includes expanded FD001/FD003 validation and executable real-data and synthetic-regression reuse examples. The source and supplementary materials are available at https://github.com/huahuobuku-design/riskselrul/tree/5a90e3140d8d9c0610b7e8f6a7121d0f6277fb32.
